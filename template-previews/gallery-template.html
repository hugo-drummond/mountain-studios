About the Gallery

Showing the Artthat Matters Right Now

Kalk Bay Gallery has been championing contemporary South African art from its home on the Cape Peninsula since 2009. We represent a curated roster of established and emerging artists whose work engages seriously with the world.

We don't follow trends. We introduce them. Every artist we show has been chosen because their practice is honest, rigorous, and undeniably South African — rooted in this landscape, these histories, these tensions.

Our programme includes solo exhibitions, group shows, artist residencies, and an annual open studio weekend.
