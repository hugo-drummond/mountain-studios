Our Story

Grown from
Good Roots

The Botanist was born from a simple belief: that the finest dining begins before the kitchen — in the soil, with the grower, at the source. We partner with small-scale Western Cape farms who share our obsession with quality and ethical growing.

Every dish on our menu changes with what's in season and at its peak. We don't import trends. We grow our own.

Our dining room is designed to feel like the most elegant corner of a working garden — natural textures, living plants, warm candlelight.

Amahle Dlamini
Head Chef & Co-Founder
