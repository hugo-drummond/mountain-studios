About the Studio

Built on
Principle and
Precision

Studio Ndlovu was founded in 2011 by Zanele Ndlovu, one of Cape Town's most recognised independent architects. The studio operates from a belief that good architecture is never neutral — it takes a position, responds to context, and serves the people who inhabit it.

We are a tight-knit team of eight: architects, interior designers, and project managers who take pride in being deeply involved across the full lifecycle of every project.
