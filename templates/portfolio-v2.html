About the Studio

Light is our
material

Lumière Studio was founded in Cape Town in 2016 with a single conviction: that extraordinary photography is never an accident. It is the result of deep preparation, collaborative trust, and an obsession with the quality of light.

We work with a small roster of clients each year — deliberately — so that every project receives our full attention. Whether you're a global brand or a local entrepreneur, your story deserves to be told with the same precision.
